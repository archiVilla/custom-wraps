# Fujur

Custom Paint Shop wrap for Tesla Model 3 (pre-Highland / 2023).

- File: `Fujur.png` (1024x1024 PNG, under 1 MB)
- Theme: Fújur, the luck dragon from Michael Ende's The Neverending Story
- Palette: mother-of-pearl scales, ruby eyes, gold name on the rear band
- Hood orientation (v4): snout toward the front bumper (template top = car front), eyes toward the windshield
- Previous v3 had eyes at the front of the hood (looked upside-down in the Tesla app)

Install: Tesla app Creations → Wrap → Upload, or USB folder `Wraps`.
